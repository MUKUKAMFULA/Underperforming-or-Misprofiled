# Underperforming-or-Misprofiled
This in an investigation on Martin Odegaard's general performances over the last two seasons and trying to evaluate whether or not his problem might be positional.
Martin Odegaard has been one of Arteta’s most important
players in turning Arsenal from border line mid-table to
Premier League Champions. He joined the Gunners on an
initial loan from Real Madrid in the 2021 January transfer
window, before signing permanently in the summer of that
year at a crucial point in the Arteta project. In his second
season at the club Odegaard put up 22 g/a in what was a
surprise title challenge year for Arsenal. In his third season he
was Arsenal’s player of the season as the gunners won a
record 28 Premier League games and record 89 points,
missing out on the title by just two points behind Man City.
But the two campaigns that followed have left Arsenal fans
and football critics divided on the midfielder. A mixture of
injuries and a change of roles have seen his output decrease.
His still without a doubt on of the most important players in
Arsenal’s squad but is there more that should be expected of
the Arsenal captain?

# CONCLUSION
Martin Odegaard is definitely still one of the best attacking
midfielders in Europe. The problem may lie in how he is used
with Arsenal. Odegaard could be more effective as an Central
Midfielder (Number 8) than a Central Attacking Midfielder
(Number 10). To strenghten their midfield Arsenal could look
for alternatives for the 10 position.
